Run checklists on your phone, prove the work with photos and video, and keep going without internet. Managers see results the moment they land.
```

**Keywords** (100 characters, comma-separated, no spaces):
```

```

**Description** (4000):
```
Edildy is the checklist app for field teams: cleaning crews, warehouses, retail chains, restaurants and facility services.

Your organisation plans the work in Edildy on the web. Your phone shows what is due now, where, and when. Open a checklist, answer each item, attach photos or video as proof and finish. No internet on site? Keep working — Edildy saves everything on the phone and sends it the moment you are back online.

FOR WORKERS
• Today's work at a glance: what is due now, what is next and what is done
• Step-by-step checklists with yes/no, numbers, text, choices and follow-up questions
• Photo and video evidence straight from the camera
• Works offline; changes sync automatically
• Report a problem on the spot with photos and your location
• A calendar of your upcoming runs and shifts
• Notifications when work is assigned, due or sent back for correction

FOR MANAGERS AND AUDITORS
• See completed runs, scores and problems as they arrive
• Review a run, request a correction, or audit it
• Follow your team's work across sites from the phone

Edildy is in Azerbaijani, English and Russian.

An Edildy account is created by your organisation. If your company would like to use Edildy, write to hello@edildy.com.
